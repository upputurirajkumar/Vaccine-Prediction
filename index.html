Optimal Hyperparameter Configuration

Hyperparameter Search Space Optimal Value Impact on Generalization
n_estimators [100, 200, 300] 200 Sufficient capacity to capture non-linear perception splits
max_depth [3, 5, 7, 9] 5 Constrains interaction depth to prevent idiosyncratic memorization
learning_rate (eta) [0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2] 0.05 Smooth shrinkage parameter preventing gradient overshoot
subsample [0.7, 0.8, 0.9, 1.0] 0.80 Row subsampling to inject stochastic variance resistance
colsample_bytree [0.6, 0.8, 1.0] 0.80 Forces splits on secondary perception & demographic features
